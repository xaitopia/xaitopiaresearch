Mary had always felt like an outsider in her pack. She was the only one with silver eyes, a rare trait that made her stand out among the other wolves. She was also the only one who could shift into a white wolf, a sign of purity and power. But instead of being revered, she was shunned and feared by her packmates. They called her a freak, a curse, a witch. They blamed her for every misfortune that befell the pack, from droughts to diseases. They treated her like a pariah, a scapegoat, a punching bag.

Mary endured their abuse for years, hoping that one day they would accept her for who she was. She tried to prove herself by being loyal, obedient, and helpful. She tried to hide her differences by wearing contacts, dying her hair, and suppressing her wolf. She tried to find a mate who would love her and protect her. But none of that worked. She was still alone, unwanted, and hated.

One night, she decided to run away from her pack. She packed a few belongings and sneaked out of her cabin. She shifted into her wolf form and ran as fast as she could, leaving behind her old life and her old pain. She didn't know where she was going, but she knew she had to find a new place, a new home, a new start.

She ran for hours, crossing forests, rivers, and mountains. She avoided other packs' territories, knowing that they would not welcome her either. She was tired, hungry, and thirsty, but she didn't stop, she couldn't bring herself to stop. She kept running, hoping to find a safe haven.

She finally reached a small town, where she smelled something that made her stop. It was the scent of another wolf, a male, an alpha. She felt a strange pull towards him, a curiosity, a desire. She followed his trail, hoping to catch a glimpse of him. She found him at a bar, where he was drinking and laughing with his friends. He was tall, muscular, and handsome, with dark hair and green eyes. He was wearing a leather jacket, jeans, and boots. He looked confident, charismatic, and powerful. He was everything she was not.

She watched him from a distance, feeling a mix of awe and envy. She wished she could be like him, or at least be with him. She wondered if he would notice her, if he would talk to her, if he would like her. She decided to take a risk and approach him. She shifted back into her human form and walked into the bar. She wore a simple dress, a jacket, and sneakers. She tried to look casual, confident, and attractive. She hoped he would see her, not her eyes, not her wolf, not her past.

She walked up to him and smiled. He looked at her and smiled back. He introduced himself as Antonio, the alpha of the Black Moon pack. He asked her name, where she was from, what she was doing here. She lied and said she was Mary, a traveller, a wanderer. She didn't want to tell him the truth, afraid that he would reject her or worse. He seemed to believe her, or at least didn't care. He invited her to join him and his friends, who were friendly and welcoming. He bought her a drink, a beer, and started a conversation. He asked her about her hobbies, her interests, her dreams. She told him some truths and some lies, trying to impress him and please him. He listened to her, nodded, and laughed. He complimented her on her looks, her voice, her smile. He touched her hand, her arm, her waist. He made her feel good, special, and wanted.

They talked for hours, until the bar closed. He asked her if she wanted to go with him, to his place, to his bed. She hesitated, knowing that it was wrong, dangerous, foolish. Maybe it was the beer, but she also wanted him, needed him, craved him. She flashed a shy smile and nodded her head. What will be will be, and she wanted to be with him.

He stood up and walked towards the door and she followed him. They got to his place, and he welcomed her in. It was a loft apartment with a minimalistic design. It was clear that he was a simple and powerful wolf who didn't need much to exert power. As she turned around examining the space, he pulled her close and kissed her. That single move sparked a fire in her. She had always desired to feel wanted, to feel loved and for the first time in her life, her dreams had become her reality. She kissed him back.

Locked in deep passion, she shifted into her wolf form. Revealing what she truly was. She feared he would leave, but he didn't seem bothered by that. He shifted into his wolf form with her, revealing himself to be a black wolf with red eyes. Together they made passionate love and for the first time Mary felt at home. Finally she thought she had found her home.

The morning sun touched her face, waking up. She feared the events of the previous night were all a dream. But when she turned she saw him lying beside her, handsome as the first time she set her eyes on him. He seems to sense her eyes on him as he opens his eyes as well. He gave her a smile and reached over to kiss her lips. He was sweet, he tasted sweet.

He stood from the bed and walked towards the bathroom without saying a word. He stayed a few minutes and walked out fully dressed. She asked him if he was going out and he told her he had business to attend to, he told her to feel comfortable and see his place as her place. She layed back in bed as he walked out.

A strange feeling was starting to grow inside of her, he had marked her to be his. She would have no choice but to do his bidding for his pup now grows inside of her.
